---
title: "Ship loud, fix fast"
description: "Why I launch scrappy prototypes instead of polishing in private."
date: 2026-08-20
---

Most of my best ideas survived because they met real humans early. The polished ones died in a folder.

My rule now: if a prototype can't embarrass me a little, it ships too late. Launch it, watch where people trip, and fix exactly that. Everything else is decoration.

Boring products come from boring processes. Loud ones come from Tuesdays where you just hit deploy.
