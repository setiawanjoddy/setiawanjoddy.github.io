---
title: "Why I build in public"
description: "Notes beat press releases. Receipts beat roadmaps."
date: 2026-08-02
---

Nobody trusts a roadmap. Everybody trusts a receipt.

So I write things down while the paint is still wet: what broke, what surprised me, what I'd do differently. Half of this site is that habit — lab notes, shipped things, loud opinions.

If you're building something weird, publish the mess. The right people will find you through it.
